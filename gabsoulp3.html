Extra Information

Release & Production

Soul was released in 2020 and produced by Pixar Animation Studios. It was directed by Pete Docter, who is also known for directing other Pixar films like Inside Out and Up. Due to the COVID-19 pandemic, the movie was released on Disney+ instead of theaters in many countries.

Music & Soundtrack

Music plays a very important role in the movie. The jazz performances were created with the help of real musicians, and the soundtrack was composed by Trent Reznor, Atticus Ross, and Jon Batiste. The film beautifully combines jazz music with emotional background scores.

Awards & Message

Soul won several awards, including the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature. The movie is praised for its deep message about finding purpose and appreciating life. It encourages viewers to enjoy small everyday moments instead of only focusing on big achievements.
